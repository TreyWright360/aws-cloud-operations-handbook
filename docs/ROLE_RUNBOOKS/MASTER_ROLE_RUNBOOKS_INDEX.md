## 🎯 How to Use These on LinkedIn & Job Submissions

When posting your videos and portfolio on LinkedIn or answering interviewers:
1. **Highlight the Full Lifecycle:** Don't just show code—show the **Runbook** that explains how you troubleshoot, secure, and operate the system when things break.
2. **Quote the Metrics:** Mention **Mean Time to Recovery (MTTR)**, **Zero Data Loss SLAs**, and **Automated Rollback Safeguards**.
3. **Link to GitHub:** Point hiring managers to `https://github.com/TreyWright360/aws-cloud-operations-handbook`.
